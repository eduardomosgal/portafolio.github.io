About Me

I’m a multidisciplinary Product Designer who enjoys bringing structure to complexity. With experience in digital products and design systems, I focus on creating experiences that feel intuitive, accessible, and purposeful — not just visually appealing.

I’m driven by a simple goal: making products that are genuinely useful, intuitive, and seamlessly integrated into people’s daily lives. I love taking complex problems and simplifying them into practical, high-impact solutions. Off-screen, I stay curious by experimenting with AI tools, bridging physical prototyping with digital design, drawing, and customizing die-cast cars—always finding better, more efficient ways to build.
